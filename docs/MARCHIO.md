# The Repobay brand: technical workshop × lively market

**The idea:** the place where code becomes real work. A workshop's precision (drawings, measurements, numbered parts) together with a market's life (colours, things changing hands, people). Nothing that looks like a "dark SaaS template": no huge white-and-grey headline on black, no pill with a green dot, no rows of three identical cards.

## Colours (black + purple, since 2026-10-11)

The brand is **black and purple**. Every other colour stays within that palette (violets, lilac, orchid pink).

| `--carta` | `#FDFCFF` | `#17131F` | cards, inputs |

- **Surfaces alternate.** Black sections (`class="notte"`: hero, the payment split, the crypto band, the closing call to action, header and footer) alternate with lilac paper sections.
  - The tokens change on their own inside `.notte`, so components don't need a dark version.
  - The plates (3D figures in `.cornice`, the split receipt) stay paper even on black, like drawings pinned to a blackboard.
- **Buttons:** purple with white text; on black the hard shadow is light.
- **Highlighter:** on paper it's a band under the word; on black it's a full block in orchid (or the role colour) with black text.
- **Roles always have the same colours:** iris for the builder, orchid for the installer, lavender for the business. Purple is for the platform and the calls to action.
- **Statuses** (ok, waiting, error) keep green, amber and red: they are functional, not brand. Contrast pass of 2026-10-12: `--ok` `#0B7A55`, `--attesa` `#8F5E05`, `--errore` `#CC3517` on paper (brighter variants inside `.notte`); secondary ink `--inchiostro-3` / `--spento` is `#6F6782` (the old `#8B839C` was under 4.5:1).
- **Text on a role colour** uses `--su-ruolo`: white on iris and purple, black on orchid and lavender. Never `--inchiostro`/`--carta` on a sticker, because those flip inside `.notte`.
- **No gradients:** solid colours only.

## Logo

**The mark: the shop door (chosen 2026-10-25, "Insegna").** A thick lintel, like the sign above a shop entrance, two jambs, and inside, on the floor, a purple square: the program entering the business. The door is the customer (the bar, the shop, the salon); the square is the open-source software that arrives there. Symmetric and heavy, so it reads as an institution; still no letters (a letter-based mark was rejected on 2026-10-11). It replaced the earlier "bay and box" mark, which read as a C at small sizes.

- **Files:** `web/marchio/` (generated by `strumenti/marchio/logo.py` + `png.mjs`; served by the site, so they double as a press kit; the rules are in `web/marchio/README.md`).
  - `simbolo.svg` door in ink + square in purple (the everyday mark on paper); `simbolo-chiaro.svg` white door + night purple for dark surfaces.
  - `simbolo-mono*.svg` one colour (`simbolo-mono.svg` uses currentColor for inline use).
  - `simbolo-piatto.svg` the tile: purple rounded square (radius 22), white mark at 74%. It is the favicon (`web/icona.svg`) and the app icon (`icona-512.png`, `icona-1024.png`); `simbolo-piatto-notte.svg` is the ink tile.
  - `avatar.svg` / `instagram-1080.png` the square avatar: tile without corners, mark at 66% so the round crop keeps it whole.
  - `logo.svg` / `logo-chiaro.svg` / `logo-mono.svg` horizontal lockups, `logo-impilato*.svg` stacked; PNGs of each.
- **Wordmark:** "Repobay" in Mona Sans 800, width 110, letter-spacing −0.025em, outlined as paths (HarfBuzz shaping, kerning included): the files depend on no installed font. The variable TTF and its OFL licence sit in `strumenti/marchio/`.
- **Geometry:** 100-unit square; lintel from y 10 to 32, jambs 14 wide (x 12–26 and 74–88) down to y 90; square 24 at x 38–62, y 66–90, flush with the floor. In the horizontal lockup the word's cap height equals the door's opening (58 units): cap top on the underside of the lintel, baseline on the threshold, 26 units of air between mark and word.
- **Use:** the header shows `simbolo.svg` and the black footer `simbolo-chiaro.svg`, both at 30 px. Other `APP_NOME` values fall back to a lettered badge. On photographs use the tile, never the bare mark.
- **Clear space:** at least the lintel's thickness (22 units) on every side. **Minimum size:** 16 px for the symbol and the tile, 90 px wide for the horizontal logo.

## Type

- **Headlines:** Bricolage Grotesque (`web/font/`), weight 700–800, optical size at maximum, slightly condensed width (`font-stretch: 85%`), negative letter-spacing. Large and dense, with variety: one key word can be highlighted with a coloured background shape behind it (like a marker), never with a gradient.
- **Body text:** Geist 400/500, 16–17 px, line-height 1.6.
- **Technical labels:** JetBrains Mono 500, uppercase, 11–12 px, letter-spacing 0.08em.
  - Used for section numbering (`01 — HOW IT WORKS`), figures, measurements and notes.
  - Prices and quantities also go in mono.

## Shapes and details

- **Cards:** white background, a solid 1.5 px ink border, **small radius (4 px)**, technical.
- **Stickers (chips, labels):** round radius and full colour, they're the "market" part.
- **Hard shadow:** `4px 4px 0 var(--inchiostro)`, offset with no blur.
  - It goes on primary buttons and on hover over cards; on hover the element moves up and to the left and the shadow grows.
  - Never soft, never glowing.
- **Graph paper:** a 24 px grid in the background of the light sections; the dark sections have the grid in light ink.
- **Drawing details:**
  - **Dimension lines:** a thin line with small end marks and a measurement in mono, for example `← 30% →`.
  - **Corner marks:** crop marks at the corners of images.
  - **Numbered notes:** a circle with a number, joined by a thin line to the right spot in an illustration.
  - **Coordinates or codes** in the corners: `RB-01`, `v2.3.0`.
- **Asymmetric layout:** columns of different widths, elements that overflow the grid, 3D images that "come out" of their box. No centring everything.
- **Motion:**
  - entrances that "snap" into place (short spring);
  - button hover with the hard shadow;
  - the money flowing in the split diagrams;
  - always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 3D images (Blender)

Style: **designer toys**.
- **Materials:** matte, plasticine/vinyl with soft edges (generous bevel), the palette colours, no realistic textures, no brand text or logos (the "R" of the mark is fine).
- **Palette in Blender** (`strumenti/3d/scena.py`, `HEX`): iris builder, orchid installer, lavender business, purple accents. The product box is aubergine black (`#2A2433`) with tape in orchid and the R in purple. Everything is redone with `bash strumenti/3d/rendi-tutto.sh` (about 8 minutes; it also writes the WebP files and the social preview).
- **Light:** soft studio light, warm key light from top left, cool fill, contact shadows under the objects.
- **Camera:** isometric or orthographic at about 30°, so the objects look like pieces on a drawing.
- **Output:** PNG with a transparent background, 2400 px on the long side, saved in `web/img/`.
- **Recurring objects:**
  - the developer's laptop (code as coloured blocks);
  - the black box with the purple R (the "product" that travels);
  - the shop with the purple and white awning;
  - the toolbox (the installer);
  - stacks of coins in the three role colours;
  - generic coins for crypto (no real logos);
  - two toy figures with no face: the developer with headphones (iris), the installer with tool belt (orchid) and purple cap.
